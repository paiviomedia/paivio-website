Privacy

Paivio Media is operated by Anders Berendt Svendsen in Copenhagen, Denmark.

Contacting Paivio

The contact links open your email application. If you choose to contact us, we receive the information you send and use it to respond to your enquiry. Contact paivio.media@pm.me with questions about your information.

Website services

This website is served through GitHub Pages. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts. These providers receive technical request information when your browser loads their resources. No marketing analytics scripts are included in this website's source.

This notice covers the consultancy website. Raceform has a separate privacy policy.

Creative Snapshot requests

The form collects your name, work email, brand name and website so Paivio Media can assess your request, prepare the review and send it to you. Monthly paid social spend and your creative challenge are optional. Required fields are needed to identify the brand and contact you. Submitting does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

We process these business enquiries on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to a review you have requested and managing the related conversation (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We do not use the form for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Email delivery and spam protection

Form submissions are handled by FormSubmit and sent to paivio.media@pm.me. FormSubmit provides spam checks and documents a 30-day submission archive. Its submission flow may use Google's reCAPTCHA. See FormSubmit's privacy terms and Google's privacy policy for their processing information. These third-party services may process technical and submission information outside the European Economic Area. We do not request advertising account credentials or access through this form.

How long we keep enquiries

We keep your enquiry and review correspondence while handling the request and any conversation you choose to continue. Once that purpose ends, the information is no longer needed and should be deleted, unless retention is necessary for an ongoing client relationship, a legal obligation or a specific legal claim. FormSubmit's separate submission archive is retained for 30 days according to its documentation.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction of your information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Data portability applies where the relevant conditions are met. Contact paivio.media@pm.me to make a request. You can also complain to Datatilsynet, the Danish Data Protection Agency. These rights are subject to the applicable legal conditions.
