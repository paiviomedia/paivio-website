Contacting Paivio

The contact links open your email application. If you choose to contact us, we receive the information you send and use it to respond to your enquiry. Contact paivio.media@pm.me with questions about your information.

Website services

This website is served through GitHub Pages. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts. These providers receive technical request information when your browser loads their resources. No marketing analytics scripts are included in this website's source.

This notice covers the consultancy website. Raceform has a separate privacy policy.

Creative Snapshot requests

Creative Snapshot is currently a preview. The public form will not accept requests until email delivery is activated and tested. Please use fictitious details when testing the local preview.

When enabled, the form will collect your name, work email, brand name and website. Monthly paid social spend and your creative challenge are optional. Paivio uses these details to assess your request, prepare the review and respond to you. Submitting does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

The planned email delivery service is FormSubmit. Submissions will be sent to paivio.media@pm.me. FormSubmit documents a 30-day submission archive and provides spam checks. See its privacy terms. No advertising platform account access is requested.

Before public activation, this notice must be completed with the confirmed processing basis, provider arrangements, any international transfers and Paivio's operational retention period. To ask about access, correction or deletion of enquiry details, contact paivio.media@pm.me.
