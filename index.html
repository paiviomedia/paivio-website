The Copenhagen Method

A smarter
way to grow.

A practical rhythm of understanding, testing and learning. Clear enough to act on. Flexible enough to evolve with your business.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Start with the business, its customers and the economics behind every acquisition.

  2. 02

    Prioritise

    Identify the opportunities with the strongest potential commercial impact.

  3. 03

    Experiment

    Build and launch focused tests across media, messaging and creative.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Assess commercial impact alongside platform metrics. Make uncertainty visible.

  5. 05

    Learn

    Turn results into a shared learning flywheel that informs the next decision.

  6. 06

    Scale

    Invest in what works, with a clear view of budgets, markets and performance.
