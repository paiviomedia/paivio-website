The Copenhagen Method

A smarter
way to grow.

A practical rhythm of understanding, testing and learning. Clear enough to act on. Flexible enough to evolve with your business.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Review your business, customers, acquisition performance and commercial priorities. Identify what's working and what's holding growth back.

  2. 02

    Prioritise

    Choose the opportunities with the strongest potential impact. Focus budget, effort and attention where they matter most.

  3. 03

    Experiment

    Turn insights into structured tests across campaigns, creative, audiences and channels. Learn what actually improves performance.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Look beyond platform metrics. Evaluate results against acquisition costs, revenue and the KPIs that matter to your business.

  5. 05

    Learn

    Document what worked, what didn't and why. Use those findings to make the next round of decisions stronger.

  6. 06

    Scale

    Increase investment where the evidence supports it. Keep improving creative, channels and budgets as the business grows.
