The Copenhagen Method

The Copenhagen Method is Paivio Media’s working approach to eCommerce growth. It connects business understanding, paid acquisition, creative testing and conversion optimisation in a repeatable cycle of decisions and learning.

One commercial direction.
A connected way to grow.

By Anders Berendt Svendsen
Updated 29 October 2026

The operating rhythm

From the business question
to the next decision.

Growing an eCommerce brand involves connected choices. The message in an ad affects the people who visit. The landing page affects what they understand. Measurement affects where the next budget goes. The Copenhagen Method gives those choices a shared commercial direction, with a named priority and a clear next step.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Review the business, customers, acquisition economics and evidence behind current performance.

  2. 02

    Prioritise

    Choose the opportunities with the strongest potential commercial impact, within the team’s capacity.

  3. 03

    Experiment

    Turn the priority into a clear hypothesis and a focused creative, channel or website test.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Read results against the agreed business objective and the limits of the available data.

  5. 05

    Learn

    Record the finding, the uncertainty and what it means for the next brief or decision.

  6. 06

    Scale

    Increase investment or roll out a change when the evidence supports it. Keep monitoring as conditions change.

What this looks like
in a working partnership.

We start by agreeing the commercial goal, the available data and the resources for execution. That becomes a prioritised roadmap rather than a disconnected list of campaigns and website ideas.

Creative and media teams share observations. Website friction feeds back into acquisition decisions. Reviews produce a decision: keep testing, refine the idea, invest further or stop.

The output is a practical set of briefs, test plans, dashboards and documented learnings. The scope depends on the business. A smaller brand may need acquisition foundations first. An established brand may need more disciplined decisions across markets, creative and conversion.

Questions about the model

A few useful answers.

Is The Copenhagen Method a fixed package?

No. It is a working approach. The priorities, channels and scope are chosen around the brand’s goals, available evidence and implementation capacity.

How do the three frameworks fit together?

The Power Response Model shapes content strategy. Precision Creative Engine structures creative testing. Precision Conversion Engine structures website and funnel improvement. The Copenhagen Method connects their decisions and learnings.
