The Power Response Model

The Power Response Model is a content strategy framework developed by Anders Berendt Svendsen. It connects company goals, consumer experience, recognisable brand identity, content and channel choice. The 2027 edition updates the model for contemporary distribution and discovery.

Content people remember.
A response you can learn from.

By Anders Berendt Svendsen
Updated 29 October 2026

A connected chain
from goal to response.

First published in 2014 and rebuilt as a 2027 edition, the model starts with the business rather than the publishing calendar. Decide what the brand should mean to people, understand the context in which they meet it and choose the response the content should support.

The result is a brief that connects brand building with commercial intent, while giving creative teams room to develop a distinctive idea.

  1. 01

    Key company goals

    Agree the brand perception, business objective and commercial measure the content should contribute to.

  2. 02

    Consumer experiences

    Use reviews, comments, searches, conversations and cultural context to understand what matters to the audience.

  3. 03

    Identity repeat

    Use recognisable visual and verbal assets consistently, while adapting the execution to the context.

  4. 04

    Power content

    Design content to attract attention, stimulate interest, encourage sharing and support a relevant response.

  5. 05

    Media channels

    Choose the channels and distribution approach that fit the audience, idea and objective.

The content sequence

Earn attention.
Make the next step matter.

  1. 01

    Attract

    Build an opening that fits the platform and the audience’s context or question.

  2. 02

    Stimulate

    Give people a reason to keep watching, reading or exploring. Put the product into a relevant story.

  3. 03

    Share

    Make the idea useful, recognisable or interesting enough to pass on. Sharing is a possibility to design for, not a promised outcome.

  4. 04

    Respond

    Choose a clear next step that fits the funnel stage: explore, save, follow, enquire or buy.

Recognisable across contexts

Repeat the identity.
Keep the execution relevant.

Brand identity includes colours, typography, sound, people, language and behaviour. Repeating these cues helps connect different pieces of content to the same brand. The values should be visible in the substance of the work, alongside the logo.

Audience context changes the execution. A short-form video, an in-depth explanation and a product comparison serve different situations. The model asks what people are trying to understand and how the brand can be useful there.

AI can assist with research synthesis and variations. Clear brand rules and human review help keep that work relevant, accurate and recognisable.

Channel roles to consider when planning distribution
ChannelPotential role
Short-form videoExplore openings, ideas and cultural relevance.
CreatorsShow the product in real life through a credible individual perspective.

YouTube and podcastsProvide depth and develop a more complete explanation.

Paid mediaDistribute and test relevant creative against agreed objectives.

Agree how the work
will be evaluated.

Choose the business measure before launch. Depending on the objective, that could include new-customer revenue, retention, acquisition efficiency or evidence from a lift test. Attention and engagement help explain the response but do not independently establish incremental revenue.

Organic response can inform paid creative, but the two environments differ. A strong organic post is a useful hypothesis for a paid test rather than proof that the advertisement will perform.

The practical output is a content direction: audience insights, brand guardrails, a response objective, channel roles and a measurement plan. Precision Creative Engine can then turn that direction into a cycle of briefs and tests.

Questions about the model

A few useful answers.

What is the Power Response Model?

It is Anders Berendt Svendsen’s framework connecting company goals, consumer experiences, brand identity, content and media channels.

What does the 2027 edition add?

It addresses algorithmic distribution, search across social and AI-assisted experiences, the role of AI in production, and the need to evaluate contribution beyond last-click reporting.

How is it different from Precision Creative Engine?

The Power Response Model provides the strategic content direction. Precision Creative Engine provides the operating process for producing, testing and improving advertising creative.
