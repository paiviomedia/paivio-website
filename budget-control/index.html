Why It Matters

A useful decision layer, not just a calculator.

See pacing early

Budget issues usually show up before the month ends, but only if someone is watching the trajectory. This page turns that trajectory into a visible control point.

Compare operating assumptions

The two forecast models are intentionally simple. They make it easier to discuss whether the account is being planned from a full-month assumption or from remaining runway.

Use it as a workflow building block

This same logic can be embedded into reporting automations, Slack alerts, budget pacing dashboards, or AI-assisted media operations inside a broader Paivio workflow.
